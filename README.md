# KS19 photoionization models of MUSEQuBES CGM absorbers

80 components in 45 absorber systems (15 sightlines). Open `index.html` (static page; GitHub Pages ready).

* `velocity_plots/` - normalised HST/COS spectra (STIS for transitions redward of COS) around the H I Lyman lines and every metal transition used in the models (detections and limits), with the Voigt-profile model: individual components (coloured dashed) only in the panels of their own ion, total model of all ions (red). Vertical dashed lines mark the modelled components.
* `corner/` - posterior of the KS19 (Khaire & Srianand 2019 UVB) Cloudy photoionization model for each component: log n_H, [alpha/H], [C/H], [N/H] (independent flat priors, -3 to +1) and log N(HI); [C/alpha] and [N/alpha] are derived.
* `ionization_models/` - observed column densities (points; open triangles = 3 sigma upper limits) vs the model (red, 16-84% over the posterior); open upward triangles = lower limits (saturated); bottom panel: residual log N_obs - log N_model, cropped to +-1 dex (hollow triangles at the edge = off scale; red = model violates a limit).
* `galaxy_images/` - per system: HST cutout of the host (smallest D/Rvir within +-500 km/s; north up; arrow to the QSO; D, log M*, SFR, D/Rvir) and the MUSE white-light image with the host (yellow) and the other members of its group (cyan; group_galaxies2.dat). 3C57, HE0153, HE0238 (_rev cube), PB6291, RXSJ02282 white light collapsed from the MUSE cubes (the others do not match the catalogue astrometry).
* `final_results/` - summary figures (listed in `final_results/results.json`, shown at the top of `index.html`).
* `catalogue.csv` - posterior medians and 16-84% ranges, flags (0 upper limit, 1 detection, 2 lower limit, 6 unconstrained).

Generated by `make_gallery.py`.
